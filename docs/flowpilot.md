# FlowPilot — Product Guide

FlowPilot is the flagship product of Zentrix Labs. It turns spreadsheets into dashboards:
upload an Excel or Google Sheets file and FlowPilot suggests charts, filters and KPIs automatically.

## Pricing

- Solo plan: 19 EUR per user per month.
- Team plan: 149 EUR per month for up to 10 users.
- Enterprise plan: custom pricing, includes single sign-on and a dedicated account manager.

All plans come with a 14-day free trial. No credit card is required for the trial.

## Release history

- Version 3.0 (June 2025): new chart engine and real-time collaboration.
- Version 3.1 (November 2025): Google Sheets live sync.
- Version 3.2 (4 March 2026): dark mode and CSV export.

## Integrations

FlowPilot connects to Google Sheets, Microsoft Excel, Airtable and PostgreSQL.
Dashboards can be shared by link or embedded in Notion and Confluence.
