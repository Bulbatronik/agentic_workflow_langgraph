# Employee Handbook

## Time off

Employees get 30 vacation days per year. Unused days can be carried over until 31 March of the next year.
Sick days do not count towards vacation and require a doctor's note after 3 consecutive days.

## Remote work

Employees can work remotely 3 days a week. Tuesdays and Thursdays are office days for everyone.

## Equipment

Every new employee receives a laptop of their choice (MacBook Pro or Dell XPS) and a 300 EUR
budget for home-office equipment.

## Learning budget

Each employee has a yearly learning budget of 1,000 EUR for courses, books and conferences.
