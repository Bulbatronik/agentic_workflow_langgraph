# Customer Support

Support is available Monday to Friday, 9:00-17:00 CET, at support@zentrix.example.

## Response times

- Solo plan: within 2 business days.
- Team plan: within 1 business day.
- Enterprise plan: within 4 hours, including a phone hotline.

## Common issues

**Charts are not updating.** Check that the spreadsheet is shared with the FlowPilot
service account and that live sync is enabled in the dashboard settings.

**CSV export is missing.** CSV export was added in version 3.2. Ask your workspace admin to update.

**Forgot password.** Use the "Reset password" link on the login page. The link is valid for 30 minutes.
