# Zentrix Labs — Company Overview

Zentrix Labs was founded in 2019 in Tallinn, Estonia, by Mira Okonkwo and Jonas Feld.
The two met while working on data tooling at a logistics startup and wanted to make
dashboards that anyone could build without writing code.

## Team and office

Zentrix Labs has 42 employees. The office is on the 7th floor of the Lumen Tower in Tallinn.
The team is split into four groups: Product, Engineering, Customer Success and Operations.

## Culture

The company mascot is a purple otter named Byte. A plush Byte sits on every desk.

Every Friday at 15:00 the team holds "Otter Hour", a demo session where people show
new features and experiments to the whole company. Snacks are provided.

## Office Wi-Fi

The internal Wi-Fi network is called ZTX-Guest. The password changes every Monday and is
posted on the whiteboard next to the kitchen.
